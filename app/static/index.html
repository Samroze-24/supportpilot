Frequently asked questions

Does the AI send replies automatically?

No. Every AI reply is a draft first. An agent reviews, edits if needed, and approves it before anything goes to the customer.

Which channels are supported?

Email, live chat and WhatsApp. In this demo build, channels are simulated with sample data — no real messages are sent or received.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes, anytime from the Billing page. Usage limits are enforced per plan, so you always know where you stand.

Is my store's data kept separate from other stores?

Yes. Every store gets its own workspace, and tickets, customers, articles and billing are strictly isolated per workspace.
